Abandoned
but Not Forgotten

A treasure hunt in space. A 90-second film about the things we left on the Moon and Mars.

  1. Psst. Want to go on a treasure hunt? In space?
  2. Hold on tight. Here we go!
  3. Rockets drop their empty parts on the way up. This one is heading for the Moon.
  4. Boom! The whole Moon rang like a bell, for three hours.
  5. Treasure number one: a mirror.
  6. Number two: a family photo.
  7. Number three: a tiny statue, for brave friends who never came home.
  8. They also left golf balls, a feather, and ninety-six bags of poo. Really.
  9. Then, whoosh! They flew home, and left the bottom of their spaceship behind.
  10. Next stop: Mars!
  11. Robots floated down from the sky on giant parachutes.
  12. Meet Oppy. She was built to work for ninety days. She kept going for fourteen years!
  13. Then a giant dust storm hid the Sun.
  14. Oppy ran out of power, and went quiet.
  15. All those machines are still up there. Two hundred tonnes of them!
  16. But one treasure still works.
  17. Remember the mirror? Shine a laser at it from Earth, and the light bounces right back. Every single time.
  18. They are far away. But they are not forgotten.

One treasure still works.
